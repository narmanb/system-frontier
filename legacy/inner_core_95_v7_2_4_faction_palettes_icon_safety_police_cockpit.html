Inner Core '95 Operator Manual

The Inner Demon has corrupted the machine's directory tree. Enter each directory, recover its loose icons, and leave through the exit gate. Clean icons become credits. Infected icons disrupt your controls and are worth nothing until purified.

Other pilots belong to factions with changing relationships. Helping allies improves relations; attacking them can turn an entire faction against you. Defenders orbit icons and transfer allegiance when their icon is captured.

Some directories become races or locked duels. Black holes still lead to dragon encounters. Defeat four dragons, collect their Noble Programs, and unlock the Inner Demon directory.

Living Directory missions: rescue stranded programs, repair damaged nodes, escort couriers, scan hidden infections, and rebuild fragmented paths. Optional residents, patch vendors, quarantined caches, and fabrication salvage can appear during operations. Alloy, circuitry, and data cores unlock palettes and reusable hull stencils in Ship Factory → Library.

←/→ turn, ↑ thrust, Space fire, X secondary weapon, A ambulance, M tactical map, Esc pause.

There is no dedicated brake. Turn the ship and counter-thrust for the fastest stop. Inertia Stabilization controls gradual speed loss while coasting.

On mobile, turning stays on the left. Fire sits above the large Thrust button; Missile and Ambulance sit immediately to their left. Fullscreen keeps the phone’s current orientation. Landscape is recommended for flight, but portrait remains available.

In Strategy mode use Collect, Attack, Evade, and Exit. The ship pilots itself while you select priorities.

Mobile controls appear automatically. Landscape gives the widest playfield, while portrait uses a taller camera and is never forcibly rotated.

Paint a 24×24, 32×32, or 48×48 sprite with optional horizontal mirroring. New custom ships default to the recommended 32×32 canvas. Transparent cells are empty hull space. Ten statistics range from 1–10 and share a 50-point design budget: Acceleration, Maximum Speed, Inertia Stabilization, Turning Rate, Hull Integrity, Fuel Capacity, Fuel Efficiency, Weapon Power, Weapon Cooling and Reactor Output.

The center thruster is always free. Left and right engines cost 2 mount points each. Select a mount, choose Place Selected, then tap the exact exhaust nozzle. Modules add mass and reactor demand, while telemetry shows the resulting real performance.

Auto-Clean can preview conservative, symmetry-based or aggressive repair passes for resized artwork. Every accepted repair creates an Undo state. The optional Precision Pad provides a magnified pixel cursor, relative trackpad movement, Dab, Hold to Draw, Auto Draw and Fine movement for phone editing.

Custom ships are stored in browser storage. Exporting JSON allows backup or transfer. Imported ships are validated before being added to the library.

Ambulance: pauses the wave and offers fuel, hull repairs, missiles, laser upgrades, armor upgrades, and decontamination.

Law: deliberately destroying clean icons or attacking Enforcers creates a warrant. Interceptors may arrest you, levy fines, or confiscate equipment. Continued resistance summons a Terminator.

Browser limitation: a web page cannot silently inspect your device. Folder indexing is optional and reads only the names of files you explicitly select.
